Giving Suggestion

Apa itu Suggestion?

Ungkapan untuk memberikan ide, masukan, atau saran pemecahan masalah dalam akuntansi kepada rekan kerja/atasan.

Contoh Kalimat (Accounting Context)

Petty Cash Discrepancy

"I suggest that we check the petty cash receipts again."

Saya menyarankan agar kita memeriksa kuitansi kas kecil itu lagi.

Accounting Software

"Why don't we use MYOB Accounting software to speed up data entry?"

Mengapa kita tidak menggunakan software MYOB untuk mempercepat input data?

Financial Audit

"You should reconcile the bank statement before the auditor arrives."

Kamu sebaiknya merekonsiliasi rekening koran sebelum auditor datang.

💬 Dialogue Example: Reconciling Ledger

Rina (Junior Accountant):

"Senior, I found an error in the monthly balance sheet. Total debit and credit don't match."

Budi (Senior Accountant):

"How about re-checking the sales invoices from yesterday? Maybe some transaction entries were missed."

Rina:

"That's a good idea! I will do that right away."

Giving Opinion

Apa itu Giving Opinion?

Ungkapan untuk menyampaikan pandangan, ide, atau sudut pandang pribadi mengenai masalah keuangan/akuntansi.

Contoh Kalimat (Accounting Context)

Financial Report

"In my opinion, our net profit increased because of good cost management."

Menurut pendapat saya, laba bersih kita meningkat karena pengelolaan biaya yang baik.

Manual vs Digital

"I strongly believe that digital bookkeeping is much safer than manual paper records."

Saya sangat yakin bahwa pembukuan digital jauh lebih aman daripada catatan kertas manual.

Budget Allocation

"From my point of view, we spent too much budget on marketing last month."

Dari sudut pandang saya, kita menghabiskan terlalu banyak anggaran untuk pemasaran bulan lalu.

💬 Dialogue Example: Choosing Accounting Software

Dewi (Finance Staff):

"What is your opinion on changing our accounting system to Accurate Cloud?"

Fajar (Accounting Head):

"Personally, I think cloud accounting will make our tax reporting easier and more accurate."

Dewi:

"I agree with you! It allows us to work from anywhere as well."

🎙️ 5 Latihan Speaking Akuntansi

Dengarkan contohnya, lalu klik tombol mikrofon untuk berlatih menirukan pelafalan yang benar!

Latihan 1: Giving Suggestion Skor: -

Skenario: Kasir menemukan selisih kas kecil.

"I suggest that we count the cash box again carefully."

Artinya: Saya menyarankan agar kita menghitung ulang kotak kas dengan teliti.

Latihan 2: Giving Opinion Skor: -

Skenario: Memberikan pendapat tentang laporan rugi laba.

"In my opinion, our company expenditure is too high this month."

Artinya: Menurut pendapat saya, pengeluaran perusahaan kita terlalu tinggi bulan ini.

Latihan 3: Suggesting Solution Skor: -

Skenario: Menyarankan solusi penunggakan pembayaran faktur client.

"Why don't we send a reminder letter to the client today?"

Artinya: Mengapa kita tidak mengirim surat pengingat ke klien hari ini?

Latihan 4: Strong Opinion Skor: -

Skenario: Meyakinkan pentingnya laporan pajak tepat waktu.

"I strongly believe that paying taxes on time will avoid heavy fines."

Artinya: Saya sangat percaya bahwa membayar pajak tepat waktu akan menghindari denda berat.

Latihan 5: Audit Preparation Skor: -

Skenario: Persiapan pemeriksaan berkas fisik jurnal umum.

"How about organizing the journal vouchers in chronological order?"

Artinya: Bagaimana kalau merapikan bukti jurnal sesuai urutan tanggal?

💡 Pola / Formula Giving Suggestion

1. S + suggest + (that) + S + Verb 1

Contoh: I suggest that we audit the inventory.

2. Why don't + we/you + Verb 1 ?

Contoh: Why don't you check the balance sheet?

3. How about / What about + Verb-ing ?

Contoh: What about matching the invoices?

4. Subject + should + Verb 1

Contoh: You should print the general ledger.

💬 Ungkapan Giving Opinion

1. In my opinion, ...

Digunakan untuk pendapat umum.

2. I strongly believe that ...

Digunakan untuk keyakinan yang kuat/tegas.

3. From my point of view, ...

Menyampaikan sudut pandang profesional.

4. Personally, I think ...

Pendapat secara pribadi.
